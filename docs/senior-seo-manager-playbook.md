*Product specification foundation*

# 1. Executive overview

A senior SEO professional does not simply research keywords, write
blogs, build links or check rankings. They manage the full
organic-growth function of a business. Their work combines strategy,
technical diagnosis, content planning, authority development, analytics,
conversion optimisation and cross-functional coordination.

The AI SEO Employee should replicate this operating discipline—not
merely automate isolated SEO tasks. It should understand each website’s
business goals, maintain client-specific context, identify meaningful
changes, recommend prioritised actions, execute only within approved
permissions and measure the result.

## Core responsibilities

- SEO strategy and business alignment

- Organic performance monitoring and diagnosis

- Keyword research, search intent and keyword-to-page mapping

- Technical SEO, crawling, indexing and site architecture

- Content strategy, briefs, optimisation and quality review

- Competitor intelligence and search-results analysis

- Authority building, backlinks and digital PR planning

- Local SEO and Google Business Profile management where relevant

- AI search visibility and entity / brand presence monitoring

- Reporting, prioritisation, stakeholder coordination and verification

# 2. Daily responsibilities

Daily work is primarily about monitoring, identifying changes, resolving
urgent issues and keeping planned SEO work moving.

| **Daily workstream**              | **Typical actions**                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| SEO performance monitoring        | Review Google Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR and average position; inspect GA4 organic sessions, landing pages and conversions; identify unusual changes; distinguish normal volatility from a meaningful anomaly. |
| Keyword and visibility monitoring | Track priority keyword movements; identify pages and queries gaining or losing visibility; review keywords approaching page one; monitor branded versus non-branded search performance.                                     |
| Technical health checks           | Review indexing and crawl alerts, broken links, redirect anomalies, sitemap and robots changes, canonical issues, page availability and Core Web Vitals signals.                                                            |
| Content optimisation              | Review pages with high impressions but weak CTR; identify content decay; improve titles, descriptions, headings and internal links; flag outdated or incomplete information.                                                |
| Competitor monitoring             | Watch key competitors for notable new pages, content updates, ranking gains and changes in search-results features.                                                                                                         |
| Execution coordination            | Prepare or update briefs; coordinate with writers and developers; review completed work; verify that approved changes were implemented correctly; update task status.                                                       |

## Daily deliverable

A concise daily SEO health summary containing: what changed, why it
matters, evidence, recommended action, owner, urgency and current
status.

# 3. Weekly responsibilities

Weekly work shifts from monitoring to analysis and prioritised growth
decisions.

| **Weekly workstream**        | **Typical actions**                                                                                                                                                         |
|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Keyword opportunity research | Discover relevant queries, assess search intent and business value, identify keyword gaps and map opportunities to existing or new pages.                                   |
| Content planning             | Choose pages or articles to create, refresh, consolidate or deprioritise; prepare briefs with audience, intent, outline, entities, internal links and conversion objective. |
| Competitor analysis          | Review the most relevant organic competitors, their ranking pages, content coverage, keyword gaps and authority signals.                                                    |
| Technical SEO review         | Review crawlability, indexation, duplicate pages, redirects, site architecture, internal links and structured data issues.                                                  |
| Backlink review              | Assess new and lost links, referring-domain relevance, suspicious patterns and potential digital PR opportunities. Do not assume every low-quality link is harmful.         |
| Content audit                | Identify declining, outdated, thin, overlapping or underperforming pages and recommend refresh, merge, redirect or retention.                                               |
| Internal linking             | Find important pages with weak internal-link support and recommend contextual links from relevant pages.                                                                    |
| Local SEO                    | Where applicable, review Google Business Profile performance, local visibility, reviews, NAP consistency and location-page quality.                                         |
| Conversion analysis          | Assess which organic landing pages generate qualified leads, sales or other business outcomes—not just visits.                                                              |
| Strategy review              | Prioritise next week's work by impact, confidence, effort, dependencies and risk.                                                                                           |

## Weekly deliverables

- Weekly performance and anomaly report

- Prioritised keyword opportunity list

- Content production and refresh plan

- Technical issue tracker with severity and owners

- Competitor intelligence summary

- Backlink / authority observations

- Next-week action plan

## Questions a senior SEO manager asks

- Why did organic traffic, visibility or conversions change?

- Which pages and queries explain the largest movement?

- Which relevant keywords rank in positions 5–20 and have realistic
  upside?

- Which competitors are gaining visibility, and on which topics?

- Which high-impression pages have weak click-through rates?

- Are we attracting the right audience and generating qualified
  enquiries?

- Which action is likely to create the greatest business impact next?

# 4. Monthly responsibilities

Monthly work evaluates whether the SEO programme is improving organic
visibility and contributing to business growth.

| **Monthly workstream**  | **Typical actions**                                                                                                                                                            |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Organic growth analysis | Review organic clicks, sessions, non-brand performance, landing-page results, leads, sales, conversion rate and revenue attribution where reliable.                            |
| Comprehensive SEO audit | Crawl the site; review indexability, crawlability, architecture, redirects, canonicals, duplicate content, structured data, mobile usability and performance.                  |
| Content strategy        | Build topic clusters and a content roadmap; balance informational, commercial and transactional intent; plan new pages and refreshes; assess quality and topical coverage.     |
| Authority development   | Review backlink profile and competitor gaps; identify relevant publications, partnerships and digital PR opportunities; assess link relevance and quality.                     |
| AI search visibility    | Track brand mentions and citations in AI answer experiences where measurable; improve factual accuracy, source credibility, entity consistency and useful first-party content. |
| Next-month planning     | Set realistic objectives, allocate resources, prioritise initiatives, document dependencies and prepare the management report and roadmap.                                     |

## Monthly deliverables

- Monthly SEO performance report with business outcomes

- Comprehensive website audit and prioritised remediation plan

- Competitor and keyword-gap report

- Keyword map and content calendar

- Backlink and authority report

- AI search visibility observations

- Next-month SEO growth roadmap

# 6. Recommended multi-agent architecture

Avoid building one undifferentiated agent that attempts every SEO task.
Use a central AI SEO Manager to orchestrate specialised agents, share a
common client knowledge base and maintain a single prioritised task
queue.

| **Agent**                     | **Primary responsibility**                                                                                                    |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| AI SEO Manager / Orchestrator | Owns strategy, client objectives, context, prioritisation, delegation, approvals and final recommendations.                   |
| Keyword Research Agent        | Discovers keywords, classifies intent, clusters topics, maps terms to pages and identifies gaps.                              |
| Technical SEO Agent           | Crawls websites, diagnoses technical issues, assesses severity and prepares implementation recommendations.                   |
| Content SEO Agent             | Creates briefs, reviews content, recommends refreshes, checks intent alignment and suggests internal links.                   |
| Off-page SEO Agent            | Reviews backlink and referring-domain patterns, identifies relevant outreach and digital PR opportunities.                    |
| Analytics Agent               | Combines GSC and GA4 evidence, explains performance changes and tracks organic conversions.                                   |
| AI Search Agent               | Monitors AI answer visibility where measurable and recommends improvements to source quality, entities and brand information. |
| Local SEO Agent               | Manages local keyword, profile, review and location-page analysis where relevant.                                             |
| Competitor Intelligence Agent | Tracks competitor pages, ranking changes, keyword gaps and content opportunities.                                             |

## Operating flow

1.  Observe: collect data from connected sources and scheduled crawls.

2.  Diagnose: identify issues and opportunities, with evidence and
    confidence levels.

3.  Decide: rank work by business impact, confidence, effort, urgency
    and risk.

4.  Execute: create briefs, tickets or approved changes within defined
    permissions.

5.  Verify: confirm implementation and measure outcomes over an
    appropriate period.

# 7. Example incident workflow: organic traffic drops 20%

A useful AI employee should investigate a decline rather than simply
send an alert.

6.  Validate the decline against an appropriate comparison period;
    account for seasonality, weekday mix, tracking changes and data
    delays.

7.  Segment the decline by page, query, country, device, brand/non-brand
    and search type.

8.  Identify the pages and queries contributing most to the loss.

9.  Check indexing, crawl, canonical, redirect, robots, site-release and
    analytics changes.

10. Review SERP changes, competitor movements and relevant algorithm or
    market context.

11. Form ranked hypotheses and attach evidence, confidence and what
    would confirm or disprove each hypothesis.

12. Recommend corrective actions with impact, effort, risk, owner and
    approval requirements.

13. Execute only approved changes and log exactly what changed.

14. Monitor affected pages over a suitable period and report whether the
    expected recovery occurred.

# 8. KPIs and success measurement

| **KPI**                       | **What it measures**                                                                                   |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Organic clicks and sessions   | Search traffic trend; interpret alongside demand and seasonality.                                      |
| Non-brand clicks              | Ability to attract new audiences beyond existing brand demand.                                         |
| Organic conversions           | Enquiries, purchases, bookings or other agreed business outcomes.                                      |
| Qualified lead rate / revenue | Quality and commercial value of organic acquisition, where CRM data is available.                      |
| Keyword visibility            | Presence across strategically relevant queries; avoid treating average position as a business outcome. |
| Top-10 keyword growth         | Growth in high-visibility rankings for relevant terms.                                                 |
| Technical issue resolution    | Priority issues resolved and verified, not simply number of warnings closed.                           |
| Content performance           | Contribution of pages to visibility, engagement and conversions.                                       |
| Referring-domain quality      | Relevance and credibility of earned links, not raw link volume.                                        |
| AI search visibility          | Brand mentions/citations across a defined and repeatable set of AI queries, where measurable.          |
| Organic conversion rate       | Share of organic visits that complete the intended action.                                             |

Measurement principle: agree a baseline, conversion definitions,
attribution model and reporting window with each client. Do not promise
ranking or traffic outcomes that depend on external search systems.

# 11. Guardrails, approvals and quality controls

The AI should be proactive but operate within explicit permissions.
Suggested default controls:

- Read-only analysis may run automatically when authorised data access
  is available.

- Drafts, briefs, recommendations and tickets may be generated
  automatically but should be reviewed where appropriate.

- Require approval before publishing or materially editing website
  content.

- Require approval before changing redirects, canonicals, robots
  directives, sitemap rules, templates or site-wide technical settings.

- Never automatically delete pages, disavow links or make high-impact
  changes without qualified review.

- Keep an audit log of source data, reasoning summary, recommendation,
  approval, action, actor and verification result.

- Separate client workspaces and credentials; enforce least-privilege
  access and prevent cross-client data leakage.

- Show source, timestamp, scope and confidence for findings; clearly
  distinguish observed facts from hypotheses.

- Avoid mass-generated low-value content. Require originality, factual
  review, search-intent fit and human value.

- Provide rollback or recovery instructions for changes that can be
  reversed.

# 12. Client-specific memory and multi-site management

For agency or SaaS use, every website should have an isolated workspace
containing:

- Business model, services/products, target audience, locations and
  priority markets.

- Primary conversions, lead qualification rules and revenue goals.

- Website structure, CMS, analytics properties and connected tool
  accounts.

- Brand voice, editorial rules, compliance requirements and prohibited
  claims.

- Target keywords, priority landing pages, competitors and known
  seasonality.

- Historical reports, completed changes, experiments and outcomes.

- Approval contacts, permissions, task owners and escalation rules.

The central manager may coordinate across a portfolio, but
client-specific data, credentials, prompts, files and memory must remain
logically isolated.

# 13. Definition of a high-quality AI SEO employee

- Understands business objectives before recommending SEO activity.

- Uses first-party performance data wherever possible and cites the
  evidence behind findings.

- Explains causes and trade-offs rather than listing generic SEO
  warnings.

- Prioritises a small number of high-value actions instead of
  overwhelming users with tasks.

- Distinguishes correlation from causation and labels uncertainty.

- Creates work that writers, developers and marketers can implement.

- Respects approval boundaries and maintains a complete action history.

- Measures implementation and outcomes, then updates its
  recommendations.

# 15. Final product recommendation

Position the product as an AI employee that manages a portfolio of
websites—not as a blog generator or rank-tracking dashboard. Its
defining loop should be Observe → Diagnose → Decide → Execute → Verify.
A strong first release should reliably connect performance data, detect
meaningful issues, prioritise actions, prepare useful work and report
outcomes. Expand autonomy only after permissions, verification, logging
and client isolation are dependable.

**Document purpose:** Working blueprint for product strategy, agent
design, SOP development and engineering handoff. This is an operating
framework; actual workload and cadence should be tailored to each
website’s size, market, competition and resources.
