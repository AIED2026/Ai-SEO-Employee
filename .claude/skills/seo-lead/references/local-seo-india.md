# Playbook: Local SEO (India & UAE)

## Google Business Profile (GBP): the highest-leverage local asset
- Primary category = the core money service (e.g., "Car rental agency"); 2–4 secondary categories.
- Name exactly as on signage (no keyword stuffing: it violates guidelines and gets suspended).
- Service areas for service businesses; real address only where customers visit.
- Real photos weekly (fleet, office, team, jobs), geotagging is irrelevant, so don't bother.
- Products/Services sections filled with prices where possible.
- Posts: weekly (offers, availability, events/festivals).
- Q&A: seed real FAQs (documents needed, deposit, airport timing).
- Reviews: ask every customer via a WhatsApp short link right after service; reply to all within
  48 h, using natural wording that mentions the service and place. **Never** buy or gate reviews.
- Track: calls, website clicks, direction requests (GBP Performance), UTM on the website link
  (`?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`).

## Citations / NAP
Identical Name, Address, Phone on: website footer + contact, GBP, JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART
(B2B), TradeIndia (B2B), Yellow Pages UAE / Connect.ae (UAE), Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn,
TripAdvisor (tourism), Bing Places, Apple Business Connect. Fix inconsistencies before adding more.

## Location pages that rank (and don't get flagged as doorways)
Each page needs content a visitor in that place actually needs:
- service specifics there (pickup point, delivery time from the depot, local regulations/approvals)
- local proof: projects/customers/reviews from that area, photos
- practical info: distance/time, landmarks, parking, prices
- FAQ specific to the place
If you can't write 300+ genuinely unique words, merge the page into a regional page.

## India specifics
- Track WhatsApp clicks (`wa.me` links) and `tel:` clicks as GA4 key events; they are most conversions.
- Hindi/Hinglish queries: check GSC. Add a short Hinglish/Hindi FAQ only if the queries exist.
- Festival and seasonal content 4–6 weeks ahead of the peak (Goa: Christmas/New Year by early Nov).
- Two Goa airports: Dabolim (GOI) and Manohar International at Mopa (GOX). Treat them as separate intents.
