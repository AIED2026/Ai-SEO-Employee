# Playbook: Content (research → brief → draft → QA → publish → review)

## 1. Choose the topic (from data, not ideas)
Priority sources: GSC queries with impressions but position > 8; questions from People Also Ask,
Reddit/Quora, sales calls and WhatsApp chats (ask the client for their top 20 customer questions);
competitor pages that rank and that we lack; AI-visibility gaps.

## 2. Check the SERP before writing
Search the primary query (India location unless the market differs). Note: dominant page type
(guide, listicle, product, local pack, video), what the top 3 cover, SERP features (AI Overview,
PAA, video, local pack), and who ranks (aggregators? forums?). If the top 10 are all product or
category pages, a blog post won't rank; build or improve a commercial page instead.

## 3. The brief (`content-brief` skill writes it)
Primary keyword + 3–8 secondaries · intent · target URL (new or existing) · title + meta ·
H2/H3 outline with the question headings · answer-first snippet for each H2 · facts/data we need from
the client · internal links in and out · schema · CTA · word range (from competitors, not a target to pad).

## 4. Draft
- First 100 words: answer the query and state who it's for.
- Use the client's real inputs: prices, specs, photos, cases, quotes from their team.
- No filler intros ("In today's fast-paced world…"), no generic AI phrasing, no invented stats.
- Indian English; local units and currency.

## 5. QA before publish (all must pass)
- [ ] Matches intent of the SERP · [ ] unique title (50–60 chars) and meta (140–160)
- [ ] one H1 · [ ] answer-first blocks · [ ] facts sourced · [ ] internal links (≥ 3 in, ≥ 2 out)
- [ ] schema valid · [ ] images compressed with alt text · [ ] CTA (WhatsApp/call/RFQ)
- [ ] no cannibalisation (search the site for the same keyword first)

## 6. After publish
Request indexing (GSC URL Inspection), submit to Bing (IndexNow), add to `keywords.csv`, and
check at 14 and 28 days: indexed? impressions? position? Refresh or merge if nothing moves by day 60.

## Refresh vs new
Existing page with impressions → improve it (faster wins). Two pages competing for one keyword → merge
and 301. Only create new when no page matches the intent.

## Additions from the founders' Master Operating Checklist §5 (beyond the QA list above)
- **Before writing:** define the business objective and conversion action of the page; identify
  missing information, useful angles and original insights vs competing pages; list authoritative
  sources for every claim that needs evidence.
- **During writing:** use keywords naturally (no repetition/manipulation); support points with examples
  and evidence; keep the CTA relevant and proportionate; comply with industry requirements (profile.md).
- **Before publishing:** review the URL slug (short, descriptive, no dates for evergreen pages);
  readability, grammar and spelling; mobile layout and page functionality (open on a phone viewport);
  external references and destination URLs work; human approval obtained; **record the published URL
  and publication date** in log.md and keywords.csv.
