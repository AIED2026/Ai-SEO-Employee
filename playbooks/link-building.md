# Playbook: Links & mentions (₹0 tools budget, white-hat only)

Links still matter for Google; **mentions** (even unlinked) matter for AI engines. Build both.

## Tactics by client type
**B2B manufacturer/exporter (SIF India, SIF UAE)**
- Industry associations & chambers: EEPC India, Indian Institute of Foundrymen, FIEO, Dubai Chamber, Sharjah Chamber.
- Trade fair exhibitor pages (IFEX, The Big 5 Dubai, World of Concrete), with links from exhibitor profiles.
- Supplier directories: ThomasNet, Europages, Kompass, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Go4WorldBusiness.
- Distributor / dealer "where to buy" pages; contractor case-study co-publishing.
- Technical content others cite: load class charts, standards explainers, installation guides.

**Local (Mira)**
- Goa tourism blogs and itinerary writers (offer a genuinely useful self-drive guide or route map).
- Hotels, homestays and villas in Candolim/Calangute: referral partnerships with a link on their "getting around" page.
- TripAdvisor, local directories, Goa travel Facebook groups (as the business, helpful answers).

**Edtech (Abhyaass)**
- CA student communities, ICAI regional study circle pages, CA bloggers/YouTubers (free access for a review).
- Free tools/resources others link to: an ISA AT exam-date tracker, free MCQ sets.

**Agency (ElevateDigi)**
- Clutch/GoodFirms/DesignRush/Sortlist profiles with verified reviews.
- Founder podcasts and guest articles on Indian marketing publications.
- Original research: e.g., "AI Overview presence for Kolkata businesses, 2026" built from our own checks.

## Rules
- Never pay for links or join link schemes; never accept paid gambling or other off-topic posts on client sites.
- Prioritise relevance over DA. One link from an industry body beats 50 directory links.
- Log every acquired link in `clients/<slug>/links.csv` (date, URL, type, anchor, status).
- Check the backlink sheets from the previous SEO for spammy patterns before continuing them.
