# Playbook: Reporting

## Monthly client report (draft for founders, by the 5th)
1. **Summary in 3 lines:** what moved, why, what's next.
2. **Outcomes:** organic leads/enquiries/bookings (GA4 key events: forms, WhatsApp, calls), MoM and YoY.
3. **Visibility:** GSC clicks, impressions, CTR, average position (site and money pages); top 10
   gaining and losing queries; new keywords on page 1.
4. **AI visibility:** prompts where the brand is mentioned or cited (x/N), change vs last month,
   and the competitors and sources the engines cite.
5. **Work done:** changes shipped (from log.md) with links.
6. **Issues & risks:** technical/security findings, anything waiting on the client (access, content, approvals).
7. **Next month:** 3–5 priorities with expected impact.

Every number has a source and date range. Never round up or cherry-pick. If something went down,
say so and explain why. Clients trust honest reports.

## Weekly internal note (per client, 5 lines)
Status (green/amber/red) · biggest change since last week · alerts · top 3 actions this week ·
blockers (who owes what).

## Rank tracking without paid tools
Use GSC "Queries" with the page filter, average position for the last 7 days vs the previous 7 days.
For a target keyword with no impressions yet, record "no impressions" rather than "not ranking"
(a manual check isn't proof either way).
